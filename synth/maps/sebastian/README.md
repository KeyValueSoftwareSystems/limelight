# sebastian

Put your maps here, named after the song: `levels.map.json`, `07-voice.map.json`.

They appear on the board and in both dropdowns of the two-rooms view automatically — no
registration, discovered from disk.

Commit them. They are small, they diff, and the history is the record of how your reading of a song
improved. Set `made_by.how` honestly: `model` if a program made it, `hand-written` if you
listened and typed it, `truth` only if you verified it with the audio playing.
