# The first recipe

Six moments in, six looks out. Hand-written, argued about by eye, replaced when something looks
better. This is v0 — it exists so nobody has to guess what a moment means.

| moment | what the room does |
|---|---|
| `build` | Amber. Pulse on every beat, decay shortening as energy rises. Heads sweep, speed rising |
| `stop` | **Everything to zero.** Floods, heads, strips. Hold for `holds` seconds. Silence must look like silence |
| `drop` | White flash on the downbeat, then two colours alternating every beat. Heads open wide, strobing. Strips run the pulse |
| `quiet` | Cool blue, low. Slow drift, no pulse. Nothing moves fast |
| `spotlight` | One head warm and alone. Everything else off. The contrast carries the meaning |
| `return` | Rebuild to the previous look over two beats, rather than snapping back |

## Chapters set the floor, moments set the peaks

Between moments, the look follows the chapter: `intro` and `outro` dim and cool, `verse` gentle
with a pulse on downbeats only, `break` low and drifting, `drop` bright and busy.

## Two things the recipe must never do

- **Exceed the strobe cap.** Set in the room, not here. A recipe cannot make an unsafe show.
- **Turn a small strobe into a whole-room strobe** when there are fewer fixtures than expected.

## Who decides if this is good

Renjith. Not a vote, not an average. Bring him two versions of the same fifteen seconds and ask
which is better — that is a question humans answer reliably. "How good is this out of ten" is
not.
