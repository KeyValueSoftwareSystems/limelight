FROM THE PRODUCTION, ON THIS SONG

The title is the direction. Raga of Revenge is about blood and violence, and
the show should be built on reds - deep arterial reds, crimson, oxblood, the
orange-red of a fire rather than a sunrise. That is the world the palette lives
in.

Reds, not a red. A palette of five near-identical crimsons is as flat as a
palette of five unrelated hues; what carries a section change is the distance
between a near-black oxblood and a hot scarlet, and the temperature shift from
a blue-leaning crimson to an orange-leaning flame. Build the range inside the
family.

What sits outside the family is yours to judge, and should be rare enough to
mean something: a white for the hardest hits, a single cold colour if the song
genuinely goes somewhere cold and you want the reds to look hotter by contrast.
Spend those the way you spend a full blackout.

This overrides the general instruction to pick colours from the song's emotion
alone. Everything else in the brief stands.
