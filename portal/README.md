## The one feature that earns its place before the 18th
**Drag a moment, save.** That is it. Not an editor — one gesture.

It exists because of the demo's best beat: we play *Raga of Revenge*, say out loud that the
machine only gets about a third of it, drag two moments to where they belong, and play it again.
The room watches a machine get corrected by a human in ninety seconds — which is the whole
argument, shown rather than claimed.
