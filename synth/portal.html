Limelight

A file that says what a song does. Everything here reads that file. Pick your part — it is already running, and already missing you.

If nothing loads

One command, from the repo. No install, no network, nothing to unzip.

python3 synth/serve.py

Audio is not stored anywhere — it is generated. python3 synth/compose.py writes the song from 12 KB of code, identically every time, on every machine.

Two rules that do not bend. No song files in git, ever — we ship the generator, not the audio. And never fill a field you did not verify: an empty value is worth more than a guess, because once a guess is written down nobody can tell it apart from a measurement.
