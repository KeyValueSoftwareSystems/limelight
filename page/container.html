what the score says — never moves

Derived from three numbers, not from a list of beat times. Change the tempo and every value in this column stays exactly as it is.

what your clock says — moves with the tempo

The same beats, converted into this container's own milliseconds. This is the only place the two clocks meet.

where you are — several structures at once

A song is not one list of sections. form is a partition, so exactly one covers any bar. energy and presence are sparse and overlap it freely — a build crosses a section boundary because that is what a build does. phrase is derived from a rule, not stored.

what is coming

Boundaries matter as much as beats. Anything with lead time needs to know a section ends in 900 ms, not to discover it once it already has.
