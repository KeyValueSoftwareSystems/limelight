setup — only if something is wrong

Art-Net universes start at 0. sACN calls the same one 1, and a node's own page may say either — if nothing lights, try 0 then 1 before suspecting anything else. A node reports its address as 2.x.x.x by convention even when it lives on your normal subnet, and that reported address is usually unroutable, so always send to the one it answered from. Wi-Fi drops broadcast; use a cable.

The server holds the universe and re-sends it on its own clock, so a busy browser cannot stutter a lamp. If frames stop arriving for three seconds it blacks out rather than holding the last look — a rig left lit by a crashed page is the one failure that is not allowed to be possible.

CHANNEL WALK — for a fixture with no personality

0

Find the shutter first and park it open. On most heads the dimmer does nothing until the shutter is in its open band, so walking in numerical order makes six good channels look dead. Sweep the shutter in steps of about 16 — it is banded (closed, open, strobe slow to fast, random), not a fade.
