Things that happen inside the sections

Each of these is a claim about one moment, addressed in bars and beats. Play four seconds around it and say whether it happened. The score keeps everything it finds; this slider hides the weak ones so the list stays judgeable.

Does the song actually change at these moments?

Each button plays four seconds before the change and four seconds after it. If the score is right you will hear the song turn a corner in the middle. If nothing happens, the score invented a boundary that is not there.
